About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at MHUG, Department of Information Engineering and Computer Science (DISI) of the University of Trento, advised by Elisa Ricci and Paolo Rota. My research focuses on Deep Learning and Robotics Perception, with a particular interest in Visual Localization.

I received the M.Sc. magna cum laude in Robotics Engineering (2020) and the Ph.D. in Information Engineering (2024) from the University of Perugia, under the supervision of Gabriele Costante at ISARLab. During my studies I joined the Institut de Robòtica i Informàtica Industrial (IRI-CSIC) in Barcelona and the Autonomous Robotics Research Center (ARRC), Technology Innovation Institute (TII), in Abu Dhabi, UAE.
